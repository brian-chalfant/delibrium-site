Your own library, in your pocket

Delibrium is an iPhone and iPad app for reading and listening to the books on a Delibrium library server. Read ebooks, PDFs and comics, listen to audiobooks, and download books to keep with you.

The app comes with no books of its own. It shows what is on the server you sign in to, using an account on that server.
