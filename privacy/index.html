Delibrium Support Privacy

Privacy Policy

Last updated November 8, 2026

This policy covers the Delibrium app for iPhone and iPad, published by Brian Chalfant.

The short version

  • The app has no advertising, no analytics and no tracking.
  • The app talks to one place: the Delibrium server you sign in to.
  • We don't sell your data or share it for advertising.

Who holds your data

Delibrium is a client for a library server. Whoever runs the server you sign in to holds the data the app sends there, and decides how it is kept.

  • If you sign in to books.aggressivewiggling.com, the server suggested on the sign-in screen, we run that server and this policy applies to your data on it.
  • If you sign in to any other server, its operator is responsible for your data there. We can't see it. Ask them how they handle it.

What the app sends to your server

  • Sign-in details: your username and password when you sign in, then security tokens.
  • Reading and listening activity: your place in each book, reading sessions, ratings and reading status.
  • Things you create: bookmarks, highlights, notes, shelves, vocabulary words, reading goals, challenges and book requests.
  • Words you look up, which the server looks up in a dictionary.
  • Email addresses you enter to send a book to someone, if your server has this feature turned on.

The server also holds your account's profile, such as your name and email address, and may keep ordinary access logs, such as IP addresses. The app uses this information only to provide its features.

What stays on your device

Sign-in tokens are kept in the iOS keychain. Downloaded books, covers, your settings and changes waiting to be sent to the server are kept in the app's storage. Removing a download or deleting the app removes the copies on your device.

Other services

The app itself doesn't connect to third-party services. When you look up a word, the server at books.aggressivewiggling.com asks a public dictionary service (dictionaryapi.dev) for that word. Your account details aren't included in that request.

Keeping and deleting your data

Data on books.aggressivewiggling.com is kept for as long as your account exists. To have your account and its data deleted, open a request saying you'd like to be contacted about it. Requests are public, so please don't include personal details. We will arrange the details privately. Depending on where you live, you may also have the right to access or correct your data, and you can ask the same way.

Children

The app isn't directed to children under 13, and we don't knowingly collect their data.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, we'll update this page and the date at the top.

Contact

For questions about this policy, open a request.
