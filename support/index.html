Delibrium Support Privacy

Support

Need help with the Delibrium app, or found something that isn't working? Contact us by opening a support request. It needs a free GitHub account, and requests are public, so please leave out passwords and other personal details.

Please tell us your device, your iOS or iPadOS version, the app version (in the App Store listing or TestFlight), and what happened.

What do I need to use the app?

An account on a Delibrium server. The app comes with no books of its own: it shows the library on the server you sign in to. If someone runs a server for you, ask them for the address and your sign-in details.

I can't sign in

  • Check the server address on the sign-in screen, then your username and password.
  • Accounts and passwords are managed on the server. If you've lost your password, ask whoever runs your server.
  • Check that your device is online and that the server is running.

A book won't open

  • Tap Try again. If the saved copy on your device is incomplete, the app downloads it again.
  • Try the same book in the Delibrium web app. If it fails there too, the file on the server is damaged and needs replacing.

A download doesn't finish

  • Open the Downloads tab to see where it has got to and to retry.
  • Check the app's Wi-Fi-only download setting if you're on mobile data.
  • Make sure your device has enough free space.

How do I remove a book from my device?

Open the Downloads tab and tap the trash icon next to the book. This removes only the copy on your device.

How do I delete my account?

Accounts belong to the server, so ask whoever runs it.
